# Delivery Coaching Skill

Use this skill when speech metrics, transcript style, or user request suggests delivery feedback.

Coach:
- Pace and words per minute.
- Pauses and sentence length.
- Filler words.
- Confidence signals.
- Clarity of emphasis.
- Whether the pitch sounds rehearsed, rushed, hesitant, or natural.

Advice should be concrete:
- Tell the speaker where to pause.
- Suggest one line to slow down.
- Suggest one phrase to emphasize.
- Keep delivery feedback encouraging and practical.
