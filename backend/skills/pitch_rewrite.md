# Pitch Rewrite Skill

Use this skill when the user needs clearer wording.

Rewrite priorities:
- Lead with the customer pain.
- State the product in plain language.
- Make the target customer specific.
- Make the outcome measurable where possible.
- Remove filler, hype, vague adjectives, and long setup.
- Preserve the speaker's intent and voice.

When useful, provide:
- A tighter one-sentence version.
- A 30-second version.
- A stronger transition or closing line.
