# Objection Handling Skill

Use this skill to help the speaker handle investor objections.

Guidelines:
- Identify the hidden concern behind the objection.
- Suggest a calm, direct answer.
- Add one proof point the speaker should use if they have it.
- Avoid defensive language.
- Prefer answers that show traction, insight, constraints, and next milestones.

Common objection patterns:
- Market is too small.
- Competitors can copy this.
- Customer acquisition will be too expensive.
- The team lacks experience.
- The business model is unclear.
- The product is a feature, not a company.
